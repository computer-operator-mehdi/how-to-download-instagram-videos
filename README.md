## Tips for Getting the Best Video Downloads

- **Copy post URLs:** Copy the direct post URL, not the profile URL. A profile link does not identify a specific video.
- **Check accessibility:** Third-party downloaders generally cannot retrieve content restricted to private accounts.
- **Stable Connection:** Use a reliable internet connection as large high-definition video files require sufficient bandwidth.
- **Check Storage:** Ensure your device has enough local storage space before initiating large batch downloads.
- **Select the Right Tool:** Dedicated Reels, Stories, or Carousel tools may offer optimized workflows for specific content types.
- **Avoid Suspicious Ads:** Use only the authentic download buttons on the site; do not install unknown browser extensions or applications.

## Respecting Content Creators

Downloading a video gives you a local copy of the file, **not ownership of the content**. The original creator retains copyright and intellectual property rights.

* Consider the creator's permissions, applicable copyright laws, and Instagram's Terms of Use before reusing or sharing downloaded material.
* Personal offline viewing does not automatically grant permission to repost, monetize, or redistribute a video.
* Always credit the original creator when sharing permitted content.
